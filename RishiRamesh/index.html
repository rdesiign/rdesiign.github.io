( GOOD TO KNOW )

FAQ.

What kind of projects do you take on?

I partner with people building thoughtful brands, digital products, and experiences. Every project starts with a conversation about what you need.

How does a typical project begin?

I start with a short discovery session, define the right scope, and share a clear plan before any design work begins.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the size of the project. Most focused engagements take a few weeks; larger launches are planned collaboratively.

Can I work with you remotely?

Absolutely. I work with partners everywhere and keep things moving with thoughtful check-ins and clear communication.

What if I only need a small piece of work?

Small, well-defined projects can be a great fit. Share a little about your idea and I’ll let you know what might work.
