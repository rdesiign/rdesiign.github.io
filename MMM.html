HEADWAY

Migraine Medication Management System

Headway addresses the issue of poor medication adherence and self-management among migraine sufferers, which leads to inconsistent dosing and risk of medication-overuse headaches. A centralised digital platform paired with a smart dose tracking hardware system was introduced to automate and simplify this process, allowing users to accurately track episodes, manage prescriptions, receive reminders, and generate data, thereby improving adherence, personalising treatment, and achieving safer, more effective treatment outcomes.

Prevalence

Migraines impact ~14 % of the global population (~1.16 billion).

Impact

Nearly half of those affected experience significant disruption to their daily activities and mood.

Poor Adherence

Only ~30 % of patients follow migraine medication regimens correctly.

Need for a holistic solution

Interviews were conducted with stakeholders (patients, patient families, and healthcare professionals) to understand how migraine impacts day-to-day life and to identify barriers to successful treatment. The key challenges centred on patients’ understanding of their condition - particularly tracking migraine triggers and frequency, maintaining consistent medication adherence, and providing means for patients and healthcare professionals to know how the effectiveness of the treatment over time and to make changes accordingly.

The Concept

Prevent

A smart container that records daily preventive medication intake using a built-in weighing scale.

Record

A mobile app that tracks migraine history and medication adherence for healthcare professionals.

Relieve

A smart migraine inhaler that connects to the app and logs each use.

Thinking out loud

The brainstorming process began with rough post-it sketches, grouped by idea similarities. These clusters informed refined pencil sketches, guided by a mood board for a modern aesthetic, diverging from traditional medical design. Selected concepts were further developed through digital sketches.

Creating mockups made of card paper and foam board based on aesthetics and component size constraints gave a better idea of the overall form. The final form was selected on the basis of aesthetic guidelines established during the ideation stage, design practicality and ergonomics. The final model was 3D printed to create an aesthetic prototype of the proposed design.

Introducing Headway

Headway tackles poor medication adherence and self-management in migraine sufferers by combining a centralised digital platform with smart dose-tracking hardware.
This system automates episode tracking, prescription management, and reminders, enabling personalised treatment and safer, more effective outcomes.

Inhaler

Smart migraine inhaler that connects to the app and logs each use. Provides immediate relief tracking and medication adherence monitoring.

Medicine Container

Smart container that tracks medication inventory and reminds users when refills are needed. Features weight sensors to monitor remaining medication and syncs with the mobile app to provide timely alerts.

The Digital Experience

The colors for the interface were chosen so as to be easy on the eyes as light sensitivity is commonly associated with migraine episodes.

"A dark themed User Interface is an effective approach to ease screen viewing discomfort for people with light sensitivity."

Track

Gives a visual idea of whether the course of treatment is working through migraine episode frequency charts.

Alert

Predicts the chances of a migraine episode based on medication adherence and environmental factors.

Record

Audio recordings are a quick and less strenuous way of logging the experience of a migraine episode.

Convey

Seamless sharing of reports, logs, prescriptions and even medical records between patients and healthcare professionals.

A Day with Headway

Start your day by taking your preventive medication through the smart container which automatically logs the dosage and time for accurate tracking.

Throughout the day, the app monitors your activity levels, sleep patterns, and environmental factors that may trigger migraine episodes.

Quickly log any symptoms or early warning signs using voice recording or simple tap gestures for detailed episode documentation.

When a migraine strikes, use the smart inhaler for immediate relief while the system automatically records usage data and effectiveness metrics.

The system analyzes your medication adherence, symptom patterns, and treatment effectiveness to provide personalized insights and recommendations.

Easily share your comprehensive treatment data with healthcare providers through secure reports for better-informed medical consultations.
