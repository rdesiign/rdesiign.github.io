Semi

Beginner's Cooktop

Semi is designed to make cooking accessible and intuitive for beginners. With simplified controls, guided cooking modes, a built-in weighing scale and smart safety features, it transforms the kitchen experience for those new to cooking while maintaining professional-grade performance.

Industrial Design Visualisation CMF Design for Manufacture
Client: Klynk
Year: 2023
Duration: 6 months

A Beginner's Story

User interviews revealed that novice cooks often second-guess themselves, not just from lack of skill but from the pressure to "get it right." Recipes assume knowledge beginners don't have, turning cooking into stress rather than exploration. The opportunity is to guide them in ways that build confidence, not just give instructions.

The Concept

Recipe Library

Curated collection of beginner-friendly recipes with adaptive difficulty levels that grow with user confidence and skill development.

Guided Cooking

Step-by-step cooking assistance with visual cues and timing prompts that reduce anxiety and build culinary confidence.

Built-in Weighing Scale

Precision weighing system integrated into the cooktop surface for accurate ingredient measurement without separate tools.

Temperature Control

Intuitive temperature management with visual indicators and preset cooking modes that prevent overheating and burning.

Design Process

Research & Discovery

Extensive user interviews with cooking beginners revealed common pain points around complex interfaces, fear of making mistakes, and lack of confidence in kitchen skills.

Ideation & Concept Development

Brainstorming sessions focused on simplifying complex cooking processes while maintaining functionality. Explored various interaction models and progressive disclosure approaches.

Prototyping & Testing

Created interactive prototypes to test different guidance systems and control layouts. Iterated based on usability testing feedback from beginner cooks.

Final Product

The final Semi cooktop combines elegant industrial design with intuitive user experience, featuring a clean interface that adapts to user skill levels.
