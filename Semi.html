Semi

Beginner's Cooktop

Semi is designed to make cooking accessible and intuitive for beginners. With simplified controls, guided cooking modes, a built-in weighing scale and smart safety features, it transforms the kitchen experience for those new to cooking while maintaining professional-grade performance.

Industrial Design Visualisation CMF Design for Manufacture
Client: Klynk
Year: 2023
Duration: 6 months

A Beginner's Story

User interviews revealed that novice cooks often second-guess themselves, not just from lack of skill but from the pressure to "get it right." Recipes assume knowledge beginners don't have, turning cooking into stress rather than exploration. The opportunity is to guide them in ways that build confidence, not just give instructions.

The Concept

Recipe Library

Personalised recipe database tailored to your skill level and ingredients to reduce decision stress.

Guided Cooking

Step-by-step guidance that reduces anxiety and builds cooking confidence.

Built-in Weighing Scale

Integrated scale for accurate ingredient measurement and portion control.

Temperature Control

Automatic temperature monitoring and adjustment for consistent cooking results.

The aesthetic aimed for a “smart” look, inspired by design patterns from devices like smartphones, laptops, and smart home interfaces. Sketches and CAD were developed in parallel, guided by the mood board and manufacturing constraints.

Introducing Semi

Semi is a supportive cooking system that helps remove uncertainty, making everyday cooking feel confident and effortless.

Effortless Meal Planning

A curated recipe library tailored to skill level and available ingredients, reducing decision-making time and simplifying the start of any meal.

Automatic Temperature Control

Semi maintains accurate temperature through a probe in constant contact with the base of the vessel, while Guided Mode automatically manages heat at each step to reduce cognitive load and ensure consistent results.

Precise Ingredient Measurement

Semi comes with a built-in weighing scale, telling you exactly what to add and how much, while cooking your next meal.
